About

I am a researcher working at the intersection of theoretical physics and machine learning. My work focuses on leveraging graph neural networks and physics-informed deep learning to solve complex problems in computational physics, quantum mechanics, and materials science.

Research Areas

Graph Neural Networks

Developing GNN architectures for modeling physical systems, molecular dynamics, and quantum many-body problems with learned representations.

Physics-Informed Machine Learning

Integrating physical laws, conservation principles, and symmetries into neural network architectures for improved generalization.

Computational Physics

Applying deep learning to quantum mechanics simulations, statistical physics, and high-dimensional partial differential equations.

Selected Projects

  • Physics-Constrained GNNs for Molecular Dynamics

  • Deep Learning for Statistical Mechanics
