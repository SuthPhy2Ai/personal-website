Physics × Deep Learning Research

About

I am a researcher working at the intersection of materials science and artificial intelligence. My work focuses on developing machine learning methods for crystal structure prediction, materials property prediction, and accelerated materials discovery. I specialize in graph neural networks, physics-informed deep learning, and active learning strategies for computational materials science.

Research Areas

Machine Learning for Materials Science

Developing AI-driven approaches for crystal structure prediction, materials property prediction, and accelerated materials discovery using deep learning and active learning strategies.

Graph Neural Networks for Crystals

Creating specialized GNN architectures like ASUGNN for crystal property prediction, incorporating crystallographic symmetries and geometric features into neural network designs.

Physics-Informed Deep Learning

Integrating physical laws and domain knowledge into neural networks for structure reconstruction from diffraction patterns, learning physics laws, and computational materials design.

Featured Publications

Physics-guided deep learning strategy for 2D structure reconstruction from diffraction patterns
Fu, R., Su, T., Li, M., Wu, Y., et al. (2025)
Introduced DD2D, a physics-guided deep learning method that predicts 2D structures directly from diffraction patterns. The model employs a twin-tower framework with crystallographic geometric encoder and site texture encoder, achieving 99.0% prediction accuracy with robust anti-interference capabilities and reliable interpretability.
Kolmogorov–Arnold Network Made Learning Physics Laws Simple
Wu, Y., Su, T., Du, B., Hu, S., et al. (2024)
Introduced KCCP (Kolmogorov-Arnold Contrastive Crystal Property Pretraining), a novel contrastive learning framework that integrates KAN with CLIP principles. The model establishes robust correlations between crystal structures and physical properties, significantly outperforming traditional MLPs in accuracy and convergence speed.
CGWGAN: crystal generative framework based on Wyckoff generative adversarial network
Su, T., Cao, B., Hu, S., Li, M., Zhang, T.Y. (2024)
Developed CGWGAN, a novel crystal generative framework using GAN to produce crystal templates with asymmetry units, space groups, and lattice vectors. Successfully discovered seven novel crystals in the Ba-Ru-O system, providing a knowledge-guided AI framework for accelerating crystal discovery.
