About

Hi! I'm a PhD candidate at Shanghai University, where I spend my days teaching computers to understand crystals (yes, they're surprisingly stubborn learners). Here's what I'm up to:

🔬 The Mission: Bridging physics and AI to discover new materials faster than traditional methods. Think of it as giving scientists a crystal ball (pun intended) to predict material properties before spending months in the lab.

🧠 The Approach: I build graph neural networks that actually understand crystallographic symmetries—not just memorizing patterns like a parrot, but genuinely learning the physics. It's like teaching AI to think like a physicist, minus the coffee addiction.

🎯 The Goal: Autonomous materials discovery. Imagine AI exploring vast chemical spaces to find next-generation superconductors, batteries, or catalysts while you sleep. We're not quite there yet, but we're getting close!

📚 The Background: Master's in Physics + current PhD in Materials Informatics = the perfect excuse to combine quantum mechanics, machine learning, and way too many Python scripts.

Research Areas

Machine Learning for Materials Science

Developing AI-driven approaches for crystal structure prediction, materials property prediction, and accelerated materials discovery using deep learning and active learning strategies.

Graph Neural Networks for Crystals

Creating specialized GNN architectures like ASUGNN for crystal property prediction, incorporating crystallographic symmetries and geometric features into neural network designs.

Physics-guided Deep Learning

Integrating physical laws and domain knowledge into neural networks for structure reconstruction from diffraction patterns, learning physics laws, and computational materials design.

Featured Publications

Physics-guided deep learning strategy for 2D structure reconstruction from diffraction patterns
Fu, R., Su, T., Li, M., Wu, Y., et al. (2025)
Introduced DD2D, a physics-guided deep learning method that predicts 2D structures directly from diffraction patterns. The model employs a twin-tower framework with crystallographic geometric encoder and site texture encoder, achieving 99.0% prediction accuracy with robust anti-interference capabilities and reliable interpretability.
Kolmogorov–Arnold Network Made Learning Physics Laws Simple
Wu, Y., Su, T., Du, B., Hu, S., et al. (2024)
Introduced KCCP (Kolmogorov-Arnold Contrastive Crystal Property Pretraining), a novel contrastive learning framework that integrates KAN with CLIP principles. The model establishes robust correlations between crystal structures and physical properties, significantly outperforming traditional MLPs in accuracy and convergence speed.
CGWGAN: crystal generative framework based on Wyckoff generative adversarial network
Su, T., Cao, B., Hu, S., Li, M., Zhang, T.Y. (2024)
Developed CGWGAN, a novel crystal generative framework using GAN to produce crystal templates with asymmetry units, space groups, and lattice vectors. Successfully discovered seven novel crystals in the Ba-Ru-O system, providing a knowledge-guided AI framework for accelerating crystal discovery.
Active learning accelerates the discovery of high strength and high ductility lead-free solder alloy
Cao, B., Su, T., Yu, S., Li, T., et al. (2024)
Employed active learning with Bayesian optimization to address the strength-ductility trade-off in SAC105 lead-free solders. After three iterations, discovered a new low-silver SAC solder exhibiting 73.94±5.05 MPa strength and 24.37±5.92% elongation, representing the best comprehensive mechanical properties. All algorithms open-sourced in Bgolearn framework.
Crystallographic phase identifier of a convolutional self-attention neural network (CPICANN) on powder diffraction
Zhang, S., Cao, B., Su, T., Wu, Y., et al. (2024)
Developed CPICANN, a real-time crystallographic phase identifier using convolutional self-attention neural network. Trained on 692,190 simulated XRD patterns, achieving 98.5% accuracy for single-phase identification with elemental information and 80% accuracy on experimental patterns, significantly outperforming JADE software.
Cuprate superconducting materials above liquid nitrogen temperature from machine learning
Wang, Y., Su, T., Cui, Y., Ma, X., et al. (2023)
Investigated ML models using element symbolic and physics knowledge descriptors for cuprate superconductors. Analysis showed cuprates offer greatest potential as superconducting candidates. SHAP analysis revealed covalent bond length and hole doping concentration as crucial factors influencing superconducting critical temperature.
Physics-Based Feature Makes Machine Learning Cognizing Crystal Properties Simple
Su, T., Cui, Y., Lian, Z., Hu, M., et al. (2021)
Proposed electron probability waves (EPW) descriptor based on electronic structures extracted from high-symmetry points in Brillouin zone. Achieved 0.92 accuracy in distinguishing ferromagnetic/antiferromagnetic materials. EPW-based ML model obtained performance equivalent to crystal graph features-based deep learning models with significantly lower computational cost.
Volume-matched ferroelectric and piezoelectric ZnO/MgO superlattice
Yang, X., Hu, M., Su, T., Ma, X., et al. (2021)
Constructed stable ZnMgO superlattice using volume matching condition and calculated electronic, ferroelectric and piezoelectric properties. Found epitaxial tensile strain effectively reduces polarization barrier, increases piezoelectric constant e33. Piezoelectric modulus d33 enhanced by 4 times at 4% strain.
Ferroic properties and piezoelectric response of Mg2XN3 (X = V, Cr)
Hu, M., Yang, X., Su, T., Ma, X., et al. (2021)
Investigated ferroic properties and piezoelectric response of Mg2XN3 compounds using density functional theory. Studied electronic structure, ferroelectric polarization, and piezoelectric coefficients. Provided insights into multiferroic materials for potential applications in electronic devices.
